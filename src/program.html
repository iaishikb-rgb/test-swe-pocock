The Banking Career Program

A four-year roadmap from Kenyon to your first offer.

Year 1 — Foundations

Financial Literacy Bootcamp

Weekly workshops covering accounting basics, the three financial statements, and how to read a 10-K. No finance background required.

Year 2 — Technical Skills

Modeling & Valuation Track

Build DCF, comparable company, and LBO models from scratch. Members complete a capstone stock pitch presented to club leadership and alumni judges.

Year 3 — Recruiting

Internship Prep

Mock interviews, technical question drills, resume workshops, and 1:1 matching with alumni mentors at target banks ahead of summer analyst recruiting.

Year 4 — Placement

Full-Time Offer Support

Continued alumni networking, superday preparation, and offer negotiation guidance as seniors convert internships into full-time roles.
