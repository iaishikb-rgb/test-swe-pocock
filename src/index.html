Kenyon Finance Club

Preparing Kenyon students for careers in banking and finance.

The Kenyon Finance Club connects students with the skills, mentorship, and network needed to break into competitive banking and finance careers. We run weekly sessions on technical skills, host alumni speakers from top firms, and guide members through the internship and full-time recruiting process.

Why Join

  • Hands-on training in financial modeling, valuation, and Excel/PowerPoint
  • Mock interviews and resume reviews from upperclassmen and alumni
  • Direct connections to Kenyon alumni working in investment banking, asset management, and private equity
  • A structured path from freshman year to full-time offer
