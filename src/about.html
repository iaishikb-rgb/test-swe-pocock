About & Contact

Run by students, for students headed into finance.

Our Mission

The Kenyon Finance Club was founded to close the information gap between liberal arts students and the competitive world of banking recruiting. We believe any Kenyon student, regardless of major, can break into finance with the right preparation and network.

Leadership

Officers

The club is led by a student executive board of seniors and juniors who have completed internships in investment banking, sales & trading, and asset management, supported by a faculty advisor from the Economics department.
