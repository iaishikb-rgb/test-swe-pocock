# Kenyon Finance Club Website

The public-facing site for the Kenyon Finance Club, a student organization preparing Kenyon College students for careers in banking and finance.

## Language

**Club**:
The student organization itself — its leadership, membership, and general activities (meetings, socials, alumni relations).
_Avoid_: Organization, group (when "Club" is meant specifically)

**Program**:
The Club's structured, four-year career-preparation curriculum (Year 1 Foundations → Year 2 Technical Skills → Year 3 Recruiting → Year 4 Placement), distinct from the Club's general activities. This is what the "Program" page describes.
_Avoid_: Curriculum, track (use "Program" as the canonical term; "track" may describe a sub-part of it, e.g. "Modeling & Valuation Track")

**Member**:
Any enrolled Kenyon student who has joined the Club. No prior finance background or specific class year is required to join.
_Avoid_: Student (too broad — not every Kenyon student is a Member)

**Banking**:
Used broadly on this site to mean careers across investment banking, asset management, private equity, and sales & trading — investment banking is the flagship focus, but the Program is not IB-exclusive.
_Avoid_: Finance (too broad/vague as a page-level term; use "banking and finance careers" when the full scope is meant)
